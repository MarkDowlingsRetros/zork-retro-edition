Before you play

An unofficial fan project · version 0.4

  • Not official. This is a hobby project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft, Activision or Infocom. Zork is a trademark of its owner.
  • The game is the real thing. Zork I by Infocom (Tim Anderson, Marc Blank, Bruce Daniels and Dave Lebling), from the source Microsoft released under the MIT licence in 2025.
  • The computers are impressions. The Commodore 64, Macintosh, Apple II, IBM PC, Amiga and NeXTSTEP looks are AI-made likenesses built for fun. They are not the original systems or software, and the makers of those machines have nothing to do with this.
  • Plays Zork I. The built-in player handles the early Infocom format used by the Zork trilogy; later Infocom games will not run.
  • Saves stay in this browser. Use Export in Saves to keep a copy, and Save transcript to keep a record of your game.

Have fun, and keep an eye out for the easter eggs, both the obvious ones and the not so obvious.
